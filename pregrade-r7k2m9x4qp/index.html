      How to set the guides

      1. Drag the red handles to the card's outer edge.
      2. Drag the blue handles to the inner border (where the colored border meets the artwork frame).
      3. Fine-tune in the zoomed corner and edge panels on the right.
      4. Check the T | B and L | R readouts and each grader's centering grade.
